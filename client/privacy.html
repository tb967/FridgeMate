Privacy notice

Last updated: 12 November 2026

FridgeMate is a bootcamp demonstration project, not a commercial service. Please don't store anything in it that you wouldn't want the project team to see.

Who we are

FridgeMate was built by a two-person team as part of a coding bootcamp. For anything about your data, including deleting your account, email alyswilliams1@outlook.com.

What we store

  • Your email address and password, to let you log in. Passwords are handled by our login provider and stored in a scrambled (hashed) form, so we can never see them.
  • Your name, if you add it to your profile.
  • Your dietary preferences, if you choose to save them.
  • Your ingredients: names, quantities, units, categories and expiry dates.
  • The recipes generated for you, and which ones you've marked as favourites.

Dietary preferences are optional. Some, such as gluten-free or nut-free, could suggest a health condition, and others, such as vegan, could suggest personal beliefs. We use them only to tailor your recipes, and you can remove them from your profile at any time.

Why we use it

Only to run FridgeMate for you: to log you in, keep track of your ingredients and create recipes from them. We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't use tracking or analytics.

Who else receives it

  • Supabase provides our login system and database. Your data is stored in its Frankfurt (EU) data centre.
  • Google (Gemini API) creates the recipes. When you generate recipes, we send your ingredients (with quantities, units, categories and expiry dates), your dietary requirements, your chosen cooking time and any extra ingredients you type. We don't send your email address or name.
  • If the demo is put online, the web hosting provider runs the website and its server.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete your account, or until the demonstration is shut down, whichever comes first.

Deleting your account

Email alyswilliams1@outlook.com from the address you signed up with, and ask for your account to be deleted. We'll do it within one month. Deleting your account also deletes your ingredients, recipes and favourites.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Use the email address above. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Cookies and browser storage

FridgeMate stores your login session in your browser so you stay logged in between pages. This is needed for the site to work. We don't use any other cookies or tracking.
